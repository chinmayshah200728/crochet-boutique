📖 Zone 6 • Maker’s Nook

The Story Behind the Stitches

"Crochet by Prisha started with a simple wooden hook, a skein of pastel milk cotton, and the desire to create gifts that never fade. Every petal, bunny ear, and bow is hand-stitched one loop at a time in my Mumbai studio."

Unlike mass factory-produced plastic trinkets, handcrafted crochet carries genuine human warmth. It takes between 30 minutes to over 5 hours of dedicated handiwork to craft each piece you see here.
